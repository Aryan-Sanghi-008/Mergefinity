# Privacy Policy — Mergefinity

**Effective date:** 2026-07-26  
**Canonical URL:** https://mergefinity.app/privacy

This policy describes how Mergefinity (“the app”) handles information. No account is required to play.

## What we collect

Mergefinity is a single-player puzzle game. Game progress, settings, statistics, achievements, and purchase entitlements are stored **on your device** (local storage).

If analytics is enabled in a production build, we may collect **anonymous gameplay events** (for example: game start, win, loss, theme change, undo used, in-app purchase flow). These events do not include your name, email, or account credentials — the app does not create user accounts.

If advertising is enabled and you have not purchased Remove Ads, ad partners may process device advertising identifiers subject to your consent choice (personalized vs non-personalized) where required by law.

## Ads and consent

Ads are not shown until you have completed at least one game. In applicable regions, you may be asked whether to allow personalized ads. Declining still allows non-personalized ads unless you remove ads via purchase.

## Purchases

In-app purchases (Remove Ads, premium themes) are processed by the store (Google Play / Apple). We do not receive your full payment card details. Entitlements are restored via the store when you use Restore purchases.

## Children

Mergefinity is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for account creation because accounts are not used.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@mergefinity.app

## Changes

We may update this policy when features change. The effective date at the top will be revised; continued use after posting constitutes acceptance of the updated policy for local-only data practices described here.
