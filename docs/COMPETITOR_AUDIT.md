# Mergefinity — Competitor Audit

> P-00. Gaps and opportunities vs. four reference products. Informs differentiation; does not expand scope beyond `MERGEFINITY_GAME_PLAN.md`.

---

## Subjects

| Product | Platform / notes | Core pitch |
|---------|------------------|------------|
| **Threes!** | iOS / Android | Distinct merge rules (1+2→3, then doubles); polished art & music; intentional difficulty |
| **2048 (Cirulli)** | Web / clones everywhere | Pure open-source 4×4 merge-to-2048; the cultural baseline |
| **2048 Premium (iOS)** | iOS App Store | Paid/ad-light packaging of 2048 with themes and QoL |
| **1024!** | Mobile clones | Same family as 2048 with lower win target / variant branding |

---

## What they do well

### Threes!

- Strong brand identity and motion craft
- Clear “one more try” compulsion without cluttered chrome
- Audio/haptics feel authored, not bolted on

### Cirulli 2048

- Instantly understandable rules
- Zero friction to first move
- Universal mental model (cream board, gold 2048)

### 2048 Premium

- Themes and persistence as expected table stakes
- Cleaner than ad-laden clones

### 1024!

- Familiar loop for players who want a shorter “summit”
- Reminds that win-value is a product knob (Challenge mode in our plan uses 4096)

---

## Gaps (market)

| Gap | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| Clone fatigue | Most Android 2048 apps feel identical: stock palette, weak type, ads on every death |
| Motion quality | Slides often feel sticky or JS-threaded; merges lack tactile pop |
| Typography | Default system fonts; numerals illegible at 5 digits |
| Mode depth | Few apps ship Classic + Endless + Challenge + Time Attack cleanly |
| Achievement craft | Checklist spam or none; rare “Purist / Efficient” strategy goals |
| Accessibility | TalkBack and contrast often ignored on tiles |
| Trust | Aggressive interstitials punish first-session wins |

## Explicit non-goals (vs. competitors)

- Do not clone Threes!’s distinct merge rules — Mergefinity stays 2048-family
- Do not compete on cartoon characters or narrative campaign
- Do not add social leaderboards in P-00–P-20 unless later roadmap amends the game plan

---

## Summary

Win by being the **premium Android-first 2048**: better type, motion, themes, modes, and respectful monetization — not by inventing a different puzzle genre.
